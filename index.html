Built For Speed

Street Rush is a fast-paced arcade street racing game set in a neon-soaked city. Dodge traffic, chain drifts and push your car to the limit across a growing list of tracks.

The game runs entirely in your browser using WebGL — no downloads, no waiting. Jump in on desktop or mobile and race within seconds.

  • High-speed arcade handling with drifting and nitro
  • Multiple neon city tracks and time-of-day lighting
  • Runs smoothly in the browser on desktop and mobile
  • Instant play — no installs, no accounts, no waiting
